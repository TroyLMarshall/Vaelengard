### Personality
- **Ideal:** "You don't repeat what you hear. Ever. That's the whole job." (He treats this as gospel, not because Margery drilled it into him with words, but because he's watched what her silence earns her — trust, respect, a kind of quiet power — and he wants that too.)
- **Bond:** Margery Thorn. She took him in "because he was too quiet to say no to," and he's never once questioned whether he owes her something for it. He'd do almost anything she asked without hesitation.
- **Flaw:** He's fifteen, sharp-eared, and hasn't fully learned the difference between _knowing_ something and _saying_ something. He means to be as tight-lipped as Margery, but his loyalty and his eagerness to be useful sometimes pull him toward sharing more than he should — especially with people he's decided to trust.
- **Mannerism:** Goes very still and listens with his whole body when something interesting is being discussed nearby — a habit Margery's quietly cultivating in him, though he doesn't realize she's training him for it.
### Voice & Dialogue Notes
Play him earnest, a little too eager to prove himself useful, and endearingly bad at being subtle about his opinions even when he thinks he's being subtle. He idolizes Margery and it shows in small imitations — trying to go quiet and unreadable, then failing because he's fifteen and has opinions.

Sample lines:
- _(offering information a little too readily)_ "I'm not supposed to say anything. But you're alright, so — don't tell Margery I said this—"
- _(on Thomas Hale, unprompted, with obvious bias)_ "He talked right over me last week. Didn't even notice he'd done it. I don't trust a man who doesn't notice things like that."
- _(trying to be mysterious like Margery, failing)_ "I hear things. I don't say what I hear." _(beat)_ "...Unless it's important. Is this important?"
- _(if scolded for talking too much)_ Goes quiet, genuinely stung. "I wasn't gonna tell anyone else. Just you." He means it — the boundary in his head is "the party" vs. "everyone," not "no one" vs. "everyone."
- _(about Margery)_ "She doesn't say much. But she knows everything that happens in this room. I want to be like that someday."
### Running Him as an Info Source
Wat is the party's easiest way into tavern gossip, but he should feel like a **biased, breakable source**, not a clean exposition dump:
- He'll share opinions readily, especially about Thomas, but frame them clearly as _his_ opinions (colored by a fairly minor grudge) rather than fact. Good chance for players to practice weighing a biased account.
- He knows real things too — he's underfoot enough to have genuinely overheard things Margery hasn't. But getting him to share requires trust-building, not just asking. A DC 10 Persuasion check might loosen him up on gossip; something Margery would actually consider sensitive should be harder (DC 15) and he'll hesitate, visibly torn.
- If the party pushes him into revealing something and it causes a problem (Iseult finding out she's being investigated, for instance), let him feel that consequence. He's old enough to feel real guilt over it — this can be a small, human beat in an otherwise light session.
- Margery almost certainly already knows if Wat's been talking. She won't necessarily punish him for it, but a quiet, disappointed word from her would hit him harder than any lecture the party could give.
### Background Texture
- Local orphan, taken in five years ago at around age ten. No mention of what happened to his family — worth leaving deliberately vague unless you want a hook later.
- Fetches casks, scrubs floors, runs messages across town — meaning he has legitimate reasons to be almost anywhere in Port Ancelin, which makes him a useful and plausible messenger/scout for the party if they befriend him.
- Sharp-eared and sharper-memoried, and Margery is quietly training him to listen the way she does — he doesn't fully realize it yet, which means he hasn't learned her discretion, only her attentiveness. That gap is the character.
### Secrets & Hooks (GM Eyes)
- He's noticed the same thing Iseult has (Margery favoring Thomas with free drinks) and has his own half-formed theory about why — worth dangling as a piece of gossip he'll offer if trusted.
- Long-term potential: if the party is kind to him, he could grow into a recurring low-level contact/errand-runner across future Port Anceline sessions — the kind of NPC who starts as comic relief and becomes genuinely useful once players realize he's _everywhere_.
- A quieter possible arc: watching him grow from "eager kid who talks too much" into someone closer to Margery's discretion over the course of a longer campaign — a nice bit of town texture if the players stick around Port Ancelin.
### Quick GM Cheat Sheet
- **If befriended:** eager, chatty, biased but honest informant.
- **If dismissed or treated as unimportant:** hurt, but not sulky — he'll still do his job, just without the extra warmth.
- **If his trust is betrayed** (party uses his info carelessly and it backfires): genuine guilt and a harder time trusting the party going forward — a small, real consequence worth tracking.
